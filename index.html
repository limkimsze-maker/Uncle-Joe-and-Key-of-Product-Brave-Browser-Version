Brave Browser Edition

Help Uncle Joe rescue the villagers, solve the multiplication challenges and unlock the final door with the Key of Product.

This version deliberately removes WebGL, Three.js, 3D models and heavy lighting effects. The adventure and six multiplication stages remain.
How to play
Complete each multiplication challenge. Correct answers move you through the six chambers. Finish all challenges to collect the Key of Product and rescue the villagers.

Stages: 1-digit facts → 2-digit × 1-digit without regrouping → 2-digit × 1-digit with regrouping → 3-digit × 1-digit without regrouping → 3-digit × 1-digit with regrouping → 3-digit × 1-digit giving a 4-digit product.
